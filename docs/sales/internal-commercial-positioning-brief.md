# Managed AI Receptionist — Commercial Positioning Brief

**One-line reality:** A **managed, dedicated AI receptionist stack**—deployed and operated with **guided rollout**—not a self-serve, multi-tenant phone widget.

---

## What this is (today)

- **Managed deployment + platform hybrid:** one **dedicated environment per customer**, rolled out by a **trained operator** on a **single documented path**.
- **Pilot-first commercial SKU:** structured pilots with clear acceptance (including **live call verification**), not “sign up and figure it out.”
- **Outcome-led:** **missed-call reduction**, **after-hours coverage**, and **front-desk relief** for **phone-primary** operators.

## What this is not

- Not **low-ticket SaaS** or **mass self-serve**.
- Not “set and forget” telephony—**public URL, DNS, carrier configuration, and webhook correctness** remain part of **deployment reality**.
- Not **full custom rebrand** in the base SKU—**standard branding/config** is in; **deep white-label** is **scoped professional services**.

## Readiness level (internal)

- **Deployable and sellable as a managed pilot** with trained operators and **written scope boundaries**.
- **Support and setup burden are real**—pricing must **recover operator time**, integration work, and ongoing care.
- Broad **unmanaged** rollout at scale is **not** the current posture; **operator-assisted** is.

## Pricing posture (non-negotiable)

**Do not sell this like cheap SaaS.**  
Price for **dedicated infrastructure + guided rollout + real support**. Underpricing buys **bad logos**, **scope creep**, and **margin death**.

**Minimum viable deal structure:** setup fee + monthly pilot fee + **written scope boundary**.

## Bad deal smell (walk away or re-price)

- Wants **unlimited customization** without a cap or SOW
- Wants **bargain pricing** while expecting white-glove telephony and ops
- Has **no accountable technical / contact owner** for DNS, routing decisions, and go-live

## Best-fit customer size

- **Typically 10–80 employees**, **phone-primary**, often **one main inbound line** (or a **small** number of numbers/locations).

## Best-fit industries (hypothesis → validate in sales)

- Home services (dispatch / booking)
- Dental / clinic / med spa
- Property management
- Boutique hospitality
- Professional services (appointments, intake, routing)

## Bad-fit customers (avoid or heavy surcharge)

- Buyers who want **unlimited customization** without a **SOW**
- **Multi-site / multi-brand** rollouts **without** a single accountable IT or ops owner
- **Agency-in-the-middle** deals where design/HTML/CSS becomes endless unpaid work
- **Price-only** procurement with **no** appetite for checklists (DNS, carrier, test calls)
- Anyone expecting **Kubernetes / bespoke hosting** as the default

## Recommended pilot pricing range (current stage)

| Element | Range |
|--------|--------|
| **Setup / deployment fee** | **$5,000 – $12,000** (non-refundable or strongly non-creditable) |
| **Pilot monthly** | **$2,000 – $3,500** |
| **Pilot term** | **60 – 90 days** |

**Intent:** Respect, margin protection, and a **clean test-call / go-live** definition—not a cheap trial.

## Recommended production pricing logic

- **Annual minimum** aligned to dedicated env + support reality: roughly **$24,000 – $60,000 / year** depending on locations, numbers, hours, and included change requests.
- **Separate bucket** for deep branding, custom workflows, and nonstandard integrations (time-and-materials or fixed SOW).

## Key positioning statement (sales)

> **“We deploy a dedicated AI receptionist in your environment, lead you through carrier and go-live, and run it as a managed service—so your phones actually get answered the way your business runs.”**

## The one rule to keep in front of you

**If the deal trains the customer that deployment and telephony are free, you have already lost.**
