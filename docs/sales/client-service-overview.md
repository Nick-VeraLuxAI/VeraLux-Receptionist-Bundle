# Managed AI Receptionist — Service Overview

**Dedicated environment. Guided rollout. Outcomes you can hear on the phone.**

---

## What this is

VeraLux is a **managed AI receptionist** for businesses where **missed calls mean missed opportunities**. Each customer receives a **dedicated deployment**, **guided rollout**, and a receptionist experience **configured to the way their business actually answers the phone**—not a shared “one-size-fits-all” chatbot bolted onto a website.

Our team leads configuration and go-live so the service holds up **on real inbound calls**, where it matters most.

---

## Problems we solve

- **Missed revenue** from calls that ring out, go to voicemail, or get mishandled during busy periods
- **After-hours gaps** when customers still expect a professional answer and clear next steps
- **Front-desk workload** from repetitive questions, scheduling, and routing that should not require a human every time
- **Inconsistent experience** when different people answer with different information

---

## How it works (high level)

1. **Discovery & call flows** — We map how your business answers today: hours, services, scheduling rules, escalation paths, and what success sounds like on a call.
2. **Dedicated deployment** — We stand up **your environment** with the right configuration for your business and phone setup.
3. **Phone routing & technical setup** — We work with your team (and your phone company as needed) so calls land in the right place and stay reliable.
4. **Go-live validation** — Before we call it production, we validate the experience with **live test calls**—not only dashboard checks.
5. **Ongoing operation** — You get a **managed service** with clear boundaries: we handle the operational heavy lifting; you stay focused on running the business.

---

## Why this is not “just another chatbot”

- **Built for telephony**, not for casual website chat
- **Dedicated environment** per customer—clear ownership and predictable operation
- **Guided rollout**—you are not left alone to debug phone systems
- **Go-live discipline**—we treat **a successful real call** as the standard, not a demo script

## Pilot program (recommended first step)

**Purpose:** Prove ROI and operational fit in **60–90 days** with defined scope and a clean go-live.

### What a pilot typically includes

- Dedicated deployment and configuration for agreed call scenarios
- Guided setup for **phone routing** and **public connectivity** requirements
- **Standard branding** (business name, voice, and customer-facing presentation within our standard configuration model)
- **Test-call go-live** and focused support immediately after launch
- **Ongoing support** during the pilot term within agreed hours and channels

### Pilot pricing (indicative structure)

- **One-time setup / deployment:** typically **$5,000 – $12,000**
- **Monthly service during pilot:** typically **$2,000 – $3,500**

Final fees depend on **call volume**, **locations and phone numbers**, and **workflow complexity**. Exact fees and scope are confirmed in your agreement.

**Optional “starting at” framing (e.g. formal proposals):** lead with **starting at $5,000** setup and **starting at $2,000/month** for the pilot term, then note that final fees depend on scope.

## Standard production service (after a successful pilot)

Production is an **annual engagement** for organizations that want a **stable, managed receptionist** with predictable support.

### What production typically includes

- Continued operation of your **dedicated environment**
- **Ongoing updates** aligned to our release practices (communicated in plain terms)
- **Support** during defined business hours
- **Change management** for reasonable tuning within agreed scope (for example: hours, scripts, routing adjustments)

### Production pricing (indicative range)

Annual commitments commonly fall in the **$24,000 – $60,000** range depending on scope.

**Optional “starting at” framing:** **starting at $24,000/year** for a baseline scoped production engagement; many customers invest more based on locations, numbers, and support expectations.

**Custom branding beyond standard**, **multi-location expansion**, and **deep integrations** are scoped separately.

## Boundaries (transparent and professional)

- **Phone and technical setup is real work.** Getting calls to the right place involves **working with your phone provider**, **internet addressing (DNS) and public access requirements**, and validation—this is part of a professional rollout, not a hidden do-it-yourself step.
- **Standard branding is included; deep custom brand programs are not.** If you need **custom layout, full visual redesign, or nonstandard customer-facing portals**, we scope that as **professional services**.
- **Support is real support, not unlimited free engineering.** We define **channels, hours, and response expectations** up front so you always know how to reach us—and what is in scope.
- **Your environment is dedicated**, which is a strength: clarity and ownership are high—but it also means we price for **real infrastructure and real operator attention**.
